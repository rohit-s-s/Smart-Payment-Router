User Stories: Smart Payment Router (v1)
Saturday, November 14, 2026 · Based on Requirements Draft v1
Epic 1: Capturing a Payment
US-01: Scan a QR code As a user at a shop, I want to scan a UPI or merchant QR code with my camera or pick one from my gallery, so that I don't have to type in the payee details.
•	The app decodes the UPI string and reads the payee address, the amount (if fixed) and the merchant category code (if present).
•	If the QR has no amount, the app asks me to enter one.
•	If the QR is not a UPI string (for example, a website link), the app switches to manual entry and asks for the amount and category.
US-02: Enter a payment manually As a user shopping online or paying a bill, I want to enter the amount, category and an optional merchant name, so that I can get a recommendation for purchases that can't be scanned.
•	Amount and category are required before a recommendation is produced.
•	A manually entered purchase gets the same recommendation as an equivalent scanned one.
US-03: Confirm or change the spend category As a user, I want the app to suggest a spend category from the QR code, the merchant name or my past choices, and I want to change it, so that the recommendation is based on the right category.
•	The category comes from the merchant category code when scanning, from my selection when typing, or from my saved merchants.
•	Categories come from a fixed list (dining, groceries, fuel, travel, utilities, shopping, entertainment, other).
•	I can override the suggested category at any time.
Non-Functional Requirements to Track
Area	Requirement
Performance	Recommendation appears within 2 seconds on a mid-range Android device
Offline use	Recommendations work offline using stored rules
Accessibility	Screen reader labels on the scanner and amount input
Accuracy	Every recommendation shows its reason and reward in rupees
